Smartnote

A notepad for private records on Linux, Windows and macOS.

Smartnote keeps your notes in an encrypted storage on your computer. If you want the notes on several devices, Smartnote can back them up to a storage you choose and restore them from it. Before anything leaves your device it is encrypted with a key derived from your sync phrase, so the storage sees only ciphertext.

Features

  • Notes encrypted on the device with a key protected by your password, with a version history.
  • Local full-text and semantic search in Russian, English and code; nothing leaves the device.
  • Optional backup and restore through one of these storages:
    • your own PostgreSQL server reachable over SSH;
    • Yandex Disk, after signing in with your Yandex account;
    • Google Drive, after signing in with your Google account.
  • No accounts with the author, no servers of the author, no analytics, no ads.

Why Smartnote asks for access to your cloud storage

If you choose Yandex Disk or Google Drive as the storage, Smartnote asks for access only to its own application folder there. It cannot see, read or change any other file on your Disk or Drive:

  • Yandex Disk: the folder of the application inside Applications on your Disk (the cloud_api:disk.app_folder permission);
  • Google Drive: the hidden application data folder on your Drive (the drive.appdata scope).

In that folder Smartnote keeps one file: an encrypted container with all your notes. It uses the access only to write this backup and to read it back on your other devices. The data goes directly between your device and Yandex or Google; the author of Smartnote never receives it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
